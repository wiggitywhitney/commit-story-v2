# Wallabies

Wallabies are small to mid-sized marsupials in the family Macropodidae, native to Australia and New Guinea. They're essentially smaller relatives of kangaroos, typically standing under 1 meter tall.

## Fun facts

- A group of wallabies is called a "mob"
- They can jump up to 1.5 meters high
- Some species are nocturnal, others are crepuscular (active at dawn and dusk)
- The quokka, famous for its "smile," is actually a small wallaby
