# Naked Mole Rat Fun Facts

- **They're basically cold-blooded mammals.** Unlike nearly all other mammals, they can't regulate their body temperature and huddle together or move to warmer/cooler tunnels to thermoregulate — more like reptiles than rodents.

- **They almost never get cancer.** Their cells produce a substance called high-molecular-weight hyaluronan that prevents tumor formation. Scientists study them heavily for cancer research.

- **They live absurdly long for their size.** A similarly-sized mouse lives ~3 years. Naked mole rats can live 30+ years — the longest-lived rodent by far.

- **They have a eusocial colony structure** — like ants or bees. One breeding queen, a few breeding males, and dozens of sterile workers and soldiers. They're one of only two known eusocial mammals (the other is the Damaraland mole rat).

- **They can survive ~18 minutes without oxygen** by switching their metabolism to use fructose instead of glucose — a trick previously only known in plants.

- **Their skin lacks a neurotransmitter called Substance P**, so they don't feel pain from acid burns or capsaicin (chili pepper compound).

- **They can run backwards as fast as forwards** through their tunnel systems.

- **The queen physically grows longer** after becoming queen — her spine actually stretches to accommodate more pups.

- **Their teeth are outside their lips** (lips close behind the incisors) so they can dig without eating dirt.

- **Workers eat the queen's hormone-laden feces**, which suppresses their own reproduction — colony-level birth control.
