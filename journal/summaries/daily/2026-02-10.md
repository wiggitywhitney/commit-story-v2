## Key Decisions

- **Global Safety Net Configuration** — Implemented 27 deny rules, 33 allow rules, and 4 ask rules in `~/.claude/settings.json` rather than building complex template switching systems. Followed a "You Aren't Gonna Need It" approach, keeping it simple with sensible defaults overridable at project level.

- **Document Security Gaps Rather Than Fix Them** — Identified three non-functional deny patterns related to shell operators but chose to document them as low-risk rather than pursue complex workarounds, relying on Claude's built-in safety mechanisms as a backstop.
