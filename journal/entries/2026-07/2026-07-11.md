After some investigation with the Spinybacked Orbweaver team, they discovered the root cause wasn't code—it was that the local OpenTelemetry Collector wasn't running. The collector is what actually forwards spans to Datadog, and without it, the OTLP exports were silently failing in the background. The developer agreed this made sense: they'd probably restarted their computer on July 6th and never brought the collector back up.

Once that was sorted out, the developer realized the instrumentation agent had no forcing function to guarantee these three attributes would be included in spans. Since the Spinybacked Orbweaver dashboard queries depend on them being present (per their PRD #980), promoting them to required documents that durable contract. This ensures the instrumentation agent actually includes them, rather than treating them as optional.

The developer also discovered and fixed a broken symlink in `node_modules` that was pointing to the local commit-story source, restoring it so future commits would use the real local code instead of falling back to the published version.
